A little help, if you need it.

Why does Android ask about an unknown app?+

This app is downloaded directly, rather than through the Play Store. Android asks you to allow the browser or app you use to install it. Only allow a source you trust, and follow any additional security checks shown by your device.

What if Google Play Protect blocks the install?+

If Google Play Protect prompts 'Blocked by Play Protect', tap 'More details' -> 'Install anyway'. Direct APK installs from outside Google Play may trigger this warning; Hisaab Qitaab is completely offline and cryptographically signed.

Will updating remove my data?+

Installing a newer release over the existing app normally preserves its data. Keep the existing app installed; uninstalling it can remove local data. If Android reports a signing conflict, stop and check the download source rather than uninstalling.

The download or installation didn't work.+

Check that you have enough storage and Android 8.0 or later. Try downloading again in your phone's browser, open Downloads, and tap the APK. If you opened this page inside a messaging app, use its “Open in browser” option.
